# Cancellation Policy

## 1. Order Cancellation
Customers can cancel their order **within 24 hours** of placement without any penalty. 

## 2. How to Cancel
To cancel an order, please log in to your account dashboard and navigate to "My Orders". Select the order you wish to cancel and click the "Cancel Order" button. 
Alternatively, you can email cancellations@techgizmo.com with your order number.

## 3. Post-Dispatch Cancellation
If the item has already been dispatched:
- The order cannot be cancelled.
- You will need to wait for the item to arrive and then follow the **Refund Policy** return procedures.
- Shipping costs for the return will be the responsibility of the customer.

## 4. Subscription Cancellations
For SaaS subscriptions:
- You may cancel your subscription at any time.
- Cancellation will be effective at the end of the current billing cycle.
- **No pro-rated refunds** are issued for early cancellation during a billing cycle.
