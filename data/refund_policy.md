# Refund Policy

## 1. Overview
At TechGizmo Solutions, we strive to ensure customer satisfaction. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may be eligible for a refund under specific conditions.

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds
- **Timeframe**: Refund requests must be made within **30 days** of the original purchase date.
- **Condition**: Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition that you received them.
- **Proof of Purchase**: A receipt or proof of purchase is required.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items
Certain items cannot be refunded:
- Downloadable software products once the license key has been revealed.
- Gift cards.
- Personalized or custom-made items.

## 4. Refund Process
1.  Contact our support team at support@techgizmo.com.
2.  Once your return is received and inspected, we will send an email to notify you.
3.  If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within **5-10 business days**.

## 5. Late or Missing Refunds
If you haven’t received a refund yet:
- Check your bank account again.
- Contact your credit card company (it may take some time before your refund is officially posted).
- If you’ve done all of this and you still have not received your refund yet, please contact us.
