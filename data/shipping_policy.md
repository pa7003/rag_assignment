# Shipping Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods & Costs
We offer the following shipping methods:
- **Standard Shipping**: 5-7 business days. Free for orders over $50. Flat rate of $5.99 for orders under $50.
- **Expedited Shipping**: 2-3 business days. Cost: $12.99.
- **Overnight Shipping**: 1 business day. Cost: $25.00.

## 2. Order Processing Time
All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or holidays.

## 3. International Shipping
We currently ship to select countries in Europe and North America.
- International shipping timelines vary by destination (typically 10-20 business days).
- Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

## 4. Lost or Damaged Packages
TechGizmo Solutions is not liable for any products damaged or lost during shipping. If you received your order damaged, please contact the shipment carrier to file a claim. Save all packaging materials and damaged goods before filing a claim.
